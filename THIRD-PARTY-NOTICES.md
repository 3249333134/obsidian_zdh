# Third-party notices

This distribution bundles PDF.js (Mozilla Foundation, Apache-2.0) and Mammoth.js (Michael Williamson, BSD-2-Clause), including their transitive JavaScript dependencies. Their license texts and notices are included in THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt in the plugin package. Obsidian is a separate application and is not redistributed by this installer.

The local Diary planning interface also bundles React, React DOM, React Aria, Lucide and their included dependencies. Their license texts are appended to THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt from the actual planner build inputs. Diary components and recurrence contracts are reused from the user's local Diary project; vendor/diary-planning/SOURCE.json records the component fingerprint.

PDF.js: https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js

Mammoth.js: https://github.com/mwilliamson/mammoth.js
